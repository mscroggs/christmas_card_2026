\heading{Instructions}
\vspace{3mm}
{\footnotesize
\begin{enumerlist}
\item Solve the puzzles below. Each answer has an even number of digits.
\item Split the answer into pairs of digits. Colour in the circles containing each pair of digits. Colour in 
    the route through the maze that connects the circle containing the
    first pair of digits to the circle containing the second pair of digits (without going through
    any other circles containing digits);
    then colour the route from the second pair to the third pair;
    and so on.
For example, if a puzzle's solution is 242002, then you should colour in the circles containing
24, 20 and 02, and colour in the routes from circle 24 to circle 20 and from circle 20
to circle 02.
\end{enumerlist}}

\subheading{Blue or green}
\begin{enumerlist}
\item[1.] 1, 10 and 11 are the only positive integers less than 100 whose digits are all either 0 or 1.
How many positive integers are there that are less than $10^{17}$ and whose digits are all either 0 or 1?
\item[2.] How many positive integers are there that are less than $10^{17}$ and whose digits are all either 1 or 2?
\item[3.] There are 9 different ways to put a tennis ball and a cricket ball in three boxes labelled 1, 2 and 3.
How many ways are there to put a tennis ball and a cricket ball in 532 boxes labelled 1 to 532
such that the tennis ball is in a higher numbered box than the cricket ball?
\item[4.]
How many ways are there to put a tennis ball and a cricket ball in 39 boxes labelled 1 to 39
such that the boxes labelled 1 and 2 contain the same number of balls?
\item[5.] There are 3 ways to tile a $2\times3$ rectangle with $2\times1$ dominoes.
How many ways are there to tile a $2\times 17$ rectangle with $2\times1$ dominoes?
\item[6.] There are 3 ways to tile a $3\times4$ rectangle with $3\times1$ triominoes.
How many ways are there to tile a $3\times20$ rectangle with $3\times1$ triominoes?
\end{enumerlist}

\subheading{Red or yellow}

\item[7.] Noel writes the positive integers in a large triangle:
he writes 1;
